# Submission package

Prepared 2026-07-20. Two papers formatted for their committed primary targets.
Every requirement below was verified against the journals' live author pages in July 2026.

## logic-morality → Ergo (primary)
- **Why Ergo:** triple-anonymous review, diamond OA (no fee), no word limit, ~2-month
  first decision, explicitly open to non-traditional authors. Best structural fit for an
  unaffiliated author.
- **Fallback:** Biology & Philosophy (most natural topical home; but ≤10,000 words — this
  paper is ~11,800 rendered incl. refs, so a ~1,800-word trim needed — and OA APC unless
  the free subscription route).
- **Conference:** Marc Sanders Prize in Metaethics (next metaethics cycle: submissions
  1 Dec 2026 – 1 Mar 2027; 7,500–10,000 words incl. notes/refs, so a ~1,800-word trim is needed to enter;
  winner published in Oxford Studies in Metaethics).
- **Format applied:** anonymized PDF, no author, cleared metadata; abstract 214 words;
  author-date (Chicago) citations via generated bibliography; 12pt, 1.6 line spacing.
- **File:** logic-morality/logic-morality_Ergo.pdf

## free-speech → JESP (Journal of Ethics & Social Philosophy) (primary)
- **Why JESP:** exact scope ("moral, social, political, legal or cognate"), diamond OA
  (no fee), anonymized review, ≤12,000 words incl. notes/refs (this paper is ~9,500
  rendered incl. refs, excl. abstract).
- **Fallback:** Ergo; then Political Philosophy (the OA successor to the now-defunct
  Journal of Political Philosophy, which ceased in Jan 2026).
- **Conference:** PhilMod (Philosophy of Content Moderation; welcomes non-philosophers) /
  MANCEPT Workshops (free-speech panels; convenor CFP ~Feb).
- **Format applied:** anonymized PDF, cleared metadata; abstract 211 words
  (JESP cap 250); word count stated on
  page 1 (JESP requirement); author-date citations.
- **File:** free-speech/free-speech_JESP.pdf

## Submission mechanics
- Both journals require the initial submission as an **anonymized PDF** (Word/LaTeX only
  after acceptance). The PDFs here are that format.
- Both are **free** to submit and publish (diamond OA).
- Submit to **one journal at a time**; JESP permits simultaneous submission only if editors
  are told. List yourself as **Independent Researcher** or your actual (CS) department.
- Post-acceptance house styles differ (JESP → CMOS footnotes; Ergo → APA author-date);
  the source files regenerate to either — see build.sh.
