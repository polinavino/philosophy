# Humean Structural Realism — Metaphysics Handoff

*Companion to `probability_philosophy_handoff.md`. Records the metaphysical position developed in conversation, with the settled claims, the live forks, and the placement in the literature. Written to seed a future conversation.*

---

## 0. One-paragraph statement

The physical world **is** a single mathematical structure — not a description of one, not an implementation of one in some substrate, but the structure itself. It is a four-dimensional block, existing tenselessly and all at once (eternalism), not produced over time by laws. Laws, chances, and even determinism are not ingredients that generate the block; they are compressed *summaries* of it (Humeanism / Best Systems). Other worlds are other structures; there is no principle saying all or how many exist, and no answer to "why this one." The remaining pressure point is that almost every consistent structure is noise, so the law-likeness of *our* block is either a brute near-miracle or evidence of a simplicity-weighted ensemble — a fork left open below.

Nearest label: **Humean structural realism**, eternalist, anti-plenitude. Distinct from Tegmark (plenitude), from canonical Ontic Structural Realism (which takes modal structure as fundamental), and from Lewis (modal plenitude, worlds not identified with mathematical structures).

---

## 1. The identity thesis: the instance *is* the structure

### 1.1 "Minimal" does not mean "leanest description"

The word **minimal** was a source of confusion and must be pinned down. It does *not* mean the everyday sense — the sparest representation, the one that drops irrelevant detail. Every *representation* carries surplus: a graph of a network still has ink colour, a numeral base, a font — properties beyond the network structure it depicts. There is no such thing as a surplus-free *representation*, because representing requires a **vehicle**, and a vehicle always has properties of its own beyond the **content** it carries.

"Minimal" here names something only the world can have: **content with no vehicle.** When one quark interacts with another there is no underlying medium in which the interaction is written, no substrate bearing extra properties — "only the interaction exists." The world is structure that *obtains directly*, not structure *encoded in* anything.

### 1.2 Identity, not instantiation

Given this, the relation between world and structure is **identity**, not exemplification. There is no abstract Platonic type sitting apart that the world then *instantiates*. The word "instantiated" was rejected precisely because it smuggles in (a) a free-standing type and (b) a relation of actualisation. Instead: the world just **is** that structure.

Terminological caution: prefer *"the world is that structure"* over *"the world is the minimal specification of that structure"* — "specification" leans back toward the vehicle side (a specification is a description, with ink and base). The intended object is the *specified*, not the *specifying*.

### 1.3 Instances vs non-instances (reconciled)

- **Non-instance** = a description ("a table", "2", "a triangle") — content *with* a vehicle, or the structure considered abstractly.
- **Instance** = the structure itself, sans vehicle — the world and its parts.

Same structure, two modes: with vehicle (description) and without (world). No third thing over and above both.

## 2. Eternalism: the whole structure exists at once

The view is **eternalist** (block universe / four-dimensionalism). The object is the entire tenseless 4D structure; there is no privileged "now," no becoming, no generation.

This is what lets laws be *summaries* rather than *governors*. On a presentist/generative picture, a present slice plus laws would *produce* the next slice — laws would do ontological work. On the block picture, every event is already there, tenselessly definite; "the shape of the world across time" is simply the block being what it is, and a law is a compressed description of regularities across it.

**Crucial distinction (three separate notions):**

| Notion | What it means | What it requires |
|---|---|---|
| **Determinate** | the fact is definite in the block | eternalism — *always* holds, for every event |
| **Determined / necessitated** | the fact is fixed by other slices + laws | the laws to be deterministic |
| **Predictable** | *we* can derive it from within | determinism + knowing the laws + compute |

An event is **determinate regardless of** whether it is determined or predictable. My having these socks today is set simply by being in the block; it does not wait on determinism or on our knowing the laws. This is why "the sock-fact is already set *regardless of the laws*" is exactly right — under Humeanism the mosaic is primary and the laws supervene on it, so the laws do not set the mosaic; the mosaic sets the laws.

**Consequence for counterfactuals.** Because the world *is* the total 4D content (see §3), a "same-but-different-socks" world is not this world in an alternative state — it is a numerically *different structure*. A mathematically consistent such structure exists (e.g. insert a discontinuity where the socks change), but it is not a **model of simple, strong dynamical laws**: its Best System must either ignore a real feature or bolt on an ugly clause. So it is excluded not by inconsistency but by being *badly systematisable*. There is no *local, lawful* sock-swap; the nearest lawful variant is a wholly different mosaic (different boundary data), and the local variant is a "miracle-block" that is definable but not a world.

## 3. Grain of the structure: the total mosaic (Humean)

When we say "the world is its structure," structure = **the total concrete 4D content** — every event, the actual history, all of it — *not* merely the law-skeleton with history as one solution among many. (The two readings were labelled (a) mosaic-first and (b) laws-first; the view takes **(a)**.)

This makes the position **mosaic-first / Humean**: the total pattern is basic; laws, chances, and determinism all **supervene** on it as Best-System summaries (Mill–Ramsey–Lewis). This is the same backbone the probability handoff uses for objective chance — the metaphysics of *which structures are worlds* and the metaphysics of *chance* run on one engine.

**Flag — this is a contested side, not neutral OSR.** Canonical Ontic Structural Realism (Ladyman & Ross; French) treats the world's *modal / nomic* structure as **real and fundamental**. By pushing laws, chances, and determinism all down into supervenient summaries of a non-modal block, this view sits on the *Humean* wing of structural realism — a minority, anti-canonical-OSR position. It should be held on purpose.

Settled fork: **laws are summaries, not governors.** (The alternative — dispositional/necessitarian laws that *generate* the block — would pull back toward laws-first (b) and is rejected.)

## 4. How many worlds? Anti-plenitude and brute selection

Worlds are structures; other worlds are other (fundamental) structures — genuinely different elementary content, or none at all. The relevant space is *not* trivial counterfactuals (socks) but different **fundamental physics**. Note the physics taxonomy:

- Different initial conditions beyond the horizon (**Tegmark Level I**) — same structure.
- Different constants via different vacua (**Level II**, e.g. different cosmological constant) — arguably still *one* fundamental structure with different boundary conditions.
- Everett branches (**Level III**) — one structure; a fact about what *our* structure's dynamics do, not a plenitude principle.
- Entirely different fundamental structures / mathematics (**Level IV**) — this is the intended modal space.

**Position on quantity:**

- **No plenitude principle.** There is no reason to require that *all* possible structures exist. Some may, some may not; we know only our own. This rejects both Tegmark's egalitarian "all equally real" and Lewis's recombination plenitude.
- **"Why this structure?" is unanswered — and accepted as such.** The available deflation: there is no external *menu* of structures from which one was selected (a menu would be a set of *descriptions* — non-instances). Asking "why this one, not another" presupposes a standpoint outside the world; there is none. Not a brute primitive so much as a malformed question.
- **Modal cost:** on strict identity, the world is its structure *necessarily* — no medium exists in which it could have been otherwise. "A different world" names a different object, not this world differing.
- **The plurality does no work.** Without a plenitude principle the other structures ground nothing (they do not analyse modality the way Lewis's worlds do) and are causally/epistemically inaccessible (no shared past). So the entire content of the view is in the identity claim about *our* world plus agnosticism about the rest. Maximal ontological modesty — a feature — but the weight is all on "world = structure."

Only characterisation of the modal space available: **the mathematically consistent structures** (we have no other grip on "possible fundamental physics" — imagination fails us for exotic cases). This domain *coincides with Tegmark's Level IV domain*; the disagreement with Tegmark is therefore reduced to a single bit — the *shading* (how much of the domain is realised), not the *map*. "Or none at all" places the null structure in the space, so **"why is there something rather than nothing"** becomes one instance of the same unanswerable selection question.

## 5. The noise problem: almost all worlds are noise

This is the sharpest open pressure and the reason the "how many" question will not stay quiet.

Among all consistent 4D structures, the **compressible** ones — those with a simple, strong Best System — are vanishingly rare. Almost every structure is algorithmic **noise**: high Kolmogorov complexity, no clean laws, no persistent patterns. So on strict Humeanism + strict anti-plenitude + a single world, the pervasive law-likeness of *our* block is a **brute near-miracle**: a "randomly given" structure that turns out to be exquisitely law-systematisable. This is more galling than "why this structure," because it looks like it is *pointing at something*.

Two mechanisms suggest themselves for "selection pressure toward law-like worlds," and only one is compatible with the Humean eternalism above:

- **Generative — Smolin's Cosmological Natural Selection.** Universes literally reproduce (black holes → offspring with mutated constants); Darwinian pressure favours fecund parameter-sets. This is the literal reading of "selection *creates* more worlds," but it reinstates a **meta-time and governing meta-dynamics** — exactly the generative picture rejected *within* a world, now merely pushed up a level (with a regress: what makes the meta-laws law-like?). **Rejected** as inconsistent with the within-world Humeanism.
- **Static — a measure, not a process (Schmidhuber; "measured"/computable Tegmark).** No meta-time, nothing created; just a **measure over the ensemble weighting structures by simplicity**, ≈ 2^(−K(structure)), so law-like worlds carry almost all the mass. Atemporal, compatible with eternalism. **This is the Solomonoff/universal-prior object from the AIT thread**: "looks generated by laws" = "highly compressible" = "low K" = "high universal-prior weight." The two research threads converge on one engine.

**The bill.** A measure only *selects* if there is something real to be "more of"; a distribution over non-existent structures selects nothing. So explaining law-likeness forces the ensemble to be **real** — *measured plenitude* (many/all structures exist, weighted by simplicity). This is not Tegmark's egalitarian plenitude; it is a *principled* simplicity-weighting, which actually **trades the brute arbitrariness of §4 for a principled measure** (an improvement in one currency) at the cost of reintroducing the ensemble anti-plenitude wanted to avoid (a debit in another).

**The disciplined alternative — deflate.** A strict anti-plenitude Humean may deny there is anything to explain: "why is the block law-like?" is illegitimate for the same reason "why this structure?" is — both presuppose a sampling/standpoint the view denies. Law-likeness is then simply brute. Lean and consistent, but it leaves the coincidence unaddressed.

**Terminological correction to carry forward:** whichever horn is taken, drop "creates *more* worlds." The Humean-compatible mechanism **weights**, it does not **create** — nothing is generated over any time. Literal creation is Smolin's governing-meta-law picture, not this one.

> **Open fork (unresolved):** *Explain* law-likeness → commit to a real, simplicity-measured ensemble (= the universal prior; give up strict anti-plenitude; gain principled, non-brute selection). Or *deflate* → stay strictly single-world; law-likeness is brute like structure-selection. No explanation is available without a real ensemble to weight.

## 6. Comparison to named views (in order of similarity)

| View | Shares | Diverges |
|---|---|---|
| **Tegmark MUH, Level IV** (2008/2014) | World *is* a mathematical structure (identity, not implementation) | Tegmark asserts **plenitude** (all structures equally real); this view is anti-plenitude / agnostic. Same modal *domain*, different *shading*. |
| **Schmidhuber; computable/measured multiverse** (1997) | The simplicity-weighted measure (≈2^−K) that would explain law-likeness | Requires a *real* weighted ensemble; this view only adopts it on the "explain" horn of §5, and remains undecided. |
| **Ontic Structural Realism** — Ladyman & Ross (2007), French | "Structure is all there is"; no quiddities; relations without hidden relata; anti-Newman via *concrete* (not abstract) structure | Canonical OSR takes **modal/nomic structure as fundamental**; this view is **Humean** (modality supervenes). Opposite side of the modality question. |
| **Lewis — Humean Supervenience + Best Systems** (1980, 1994) | Laws/chances = Best System (simplicity + strength + fit) over a non-modal mosaic; eternalist mosaic | Lewis has full **modal plenitude** (concrete possible worlds via recombination) and does **not** identify a world with a mathematical structure. This view drops plenitude and adds the identity thesis. |
| **Aristotelian realism about mathematics** — Franklin (2014) | Numbers/structures real only *in* their instances ("two tables instantiate 2"); immanent, neither Platonist nor nominalist | Franklin does not make the world/structure **identity** claim, nor the eternalist-block or multiverse moves. Supplies the instances/non-instances intuition. |
| **Ante rem vs in re structuralism** — Shapiro (1997) / Hellman (1989) | The abstract/philosophy-of-math analogue: this view is *in re* about the physical (structure real through its realisation), Tegmark is *ante rem* | Concerns mathematical objects; imported here as the axis locating the view against Tegmark. |
| **Smolin — Cosmological Natural Selection** (1997) | An answer to §5's law-likeness (selection over universes) | Requires **generative meta-dynamics / meta-time** — governing laws at the multiverse level; rejected as inconsistent with within-world Humeanism. |
| **Russell / Newman** (1927/1928) | Structuralism about the physical | Newman's objection (pure abstract structure is trivially satisfiable) is *the* threat; answered here by "concrete structure, no vehicle" + Best-System lawfulness, not by abstract structure. |
| **Digital physics / "it from bit"** — Wheeler, Lloyd | Everything is structural/informational | These posit a *substrate* (bits encoded in something) — content *with* a vehicle. This view explicitly denies the substrate. |

## 7. What is settled vs open

**Settled in-conversation:**
- Identity (world = structure), not implementation-in-a-substrate; "minimal" = content without vehicle. (§1)
- Eternalist block; laws summarise, do not generate; determinate ≠ determined ≠ predictable. (§2)
- Grain = total mosaic (a), hence **Humean** structural realism, not canonical OSR. (§3)
- Anti-plenitude; "why this structure" deflated as a malformed (menu-presupposing) question. (§4)

**Open:**
- **The §5 fork:** explain law-likeness via a real simplicity-measured ensemble (→ measured plenitude; = the Solomonoff prior) vs deflate (→ brute). *This is the main unresolved question.*
- If the "explain" horn: the **measure problem** over Level IV (which measure, and does it need computability restrictions?) — inherited from Schmidhuber/Tegmark.
- Interface with the probability handoff's **Principal Principle** problem: if chance is Best-System over the block, why should an epistemic situation's credences track it? (Cross-referenced there.)
- Whether "content without vehicle" fully answers **Newman** or merely relocates it.
